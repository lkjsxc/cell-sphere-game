# src/game/skills/

Pure frozen content and physical-frontier authority for the frequency-5,
252-cell Evolution Globe.

- `index.js`: adjacency queries, purchases, compilation, preview, validation.
- `atlas.js`: six connected 42-cell territories covering all 252 cells.
- `node.js`: strong landmarks, environmental metadata, and bounded Resonance curves.
- `potential.js`: version-2 Evolution Power and monotone World Potential anchors.
- `affinities.js`: Fertility, Freshwater, Scarcity, Cryogenic, Marine, and Luminous metadata.
- `builds.js`: sixteen visible deterministic Build recipes and bounded compiled outputs.
- branch modules retain stable Reach, Flow, Reserve, Ecology, Perception, and Continuity IDs while their physical territories present the environmental affinities.
- `legacy-v4-manifest.js`: explicit 642-entry migration evidence.
- `scene.js`: semantic whole-cell renderer projection.

A purchase requires held Echoes and one directly adjacent owned geodesic cell.
The six roots are the fresh-save exception. There is no run-count, world-count,
all-parent, or hidden experience gate. Compiled output includes gameplay effects,
conditional rules, habitat capabilities, Evolution Power, versioned World Potential,
active/near Builds, Build effects, and transformations. Every detail can show power,
potential, gameplay, recipe contribution, cost, and newly available neighbors before → after.
